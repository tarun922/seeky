# Seeky Code of Conduct

Seeky's community is made up of people from all over the world — users,
people reporting bugs, and anyone who stops by to suggest a feature or help
someone else out. That mix is a strength, but it can also lead to friction,
so here are a few ground rules we ask everyone to follow. This applies
equally to the maintainer and to anyone participating as a user or
contributor.

This isn't an exhaustive list of everything you can't do. Take it in the
spirit it's intended — a guide to keep this a good place to be, for
everyone.

This code of conduct applies to all spaces used for Seeky's community:
GitHub issues, discussions, pull request comments, and any other official
communication channel the project uses. Behavior outside these spaces that
affects someone's ability to participate safely within them is also in
scope.

If you believe someone is violating this code, please report it by emailing
**[email protected]**. Reports are kept as confidential as possible.

## Expectations

- **Be friendly and patient.**
- **Be welcoming.** We want this to be a community that welcomes and
  supports people of all backgrounds and identities — regardless of race,
  ethnicity, culture, national origin, color, immigration status, social
  and economic class, education level, sex, sexual orientation, gender
  identity and expression, age, size, family status, political belief,
  religion, or mental and physical ability.
- **Be considerate.** What you post affects other people — bug reports,
  code suggestions, and comments get read and acted on by someone else.
  Keep in mind this is a worldwide community, so not everyone is
  communicating in their first language.
- **Be respectful.** Disagreement happens, and that's fine — it's not an
  excuse for poor behavior. Frustration is normal; turning it into a
  personal attack isn't acceptable. A space where people feel uncomfortable
  or unsafe isn't a productive one.
- **Choose your words carefully.** Be kind. Don't insult or put down other
  participants. Harassment and exclusionary behavior aren't acceptable,
  including but not limited to:
  - Violent threats or language directed at another person
  - Discriminatory jokes or language
  - Posting sexually explicit or violent material
  - Posting, or threatening to post, someone else's personal information
    without consent ("doxing")
  - Personal insults, especially ones using racist or sexist terms
  - Unwelcome sexual attention
  - Encouraging or advocating for any of the above
  - Repeated harassment — if someone asks you to stop, stop
- **When you disagree, try to understand why.** Technical and social
  disagreements happen; that's normal. Not being able to see why someone
  holds a view doesn't mean they're wrong. Mistakes happen — focus on
  resolving the issue, not on blame.

Adapted from the [Speak Up! project](http://web.archive.org/web/20141109123859/http://speakup.io/coc.html).

## Enforcement

Reports are reviewed by the maintainer. Depending on what happened, this
may mean a private conversation, a warning, removal of content, or a ban
from project spaces (e.g. being blocked from the repo). Since Seeky is
maintained by one person rather than a team, there isn't a separate
enforcement committee — but reports are still taken seriously and handled
as fairly and consistently as possible.
